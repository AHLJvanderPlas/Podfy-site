The Thursday afternoon problem

A supplier disputes a delivery from three weeks ago. Fourteen stores received goods from this supplier last month. Your receiving lead has signatures — on paper, somewhere. Finance is on hold pending proof.

Store staff are not archivists. A signature in a book is not a searchable record. When you have twenty suppliers each running their own POD format — email photos, carrier apps, paper notes, scanned PDFs emailed three days late — there is no single place to look when something goes wrong.

Podfy does not change what your suppliers do. It gives them one neutral link per shipment that their driver uses to submit proof. You standardise intake without forcing technology adoption on external partners.

  • One intake process regardless of carrier or supplier
  • Receiving staff confirm with a single tap — no paperwork
  • Finance searches by PO reference, date, or supplier name
  • Supplier disputes resolved in minutes, not weeks
  • No app on store or warehouse devices required
  • All records EU-hosted, GDPR-aligned, audit-ready for 7 years

How it works from the supplier side

01 — Setup

Give each supplier a link

You configure a Podfy slug for each supplier. Share the link once — in your supplier onboarding pack, your ERP, or simply by email. The supplier adds it to their dispatch process. That is the full rollout.

02 — Delivery

Driver submits at the dock

When the carrier's driver arrives at your dock, they open the link on their phone and photograph the signed delivery note. GPS confirms the location. The file lands in your portal with a timestamp — before the truck leaves.

03 — Finance

One portal for all suppliers

Your team searches by PO reference, date, or supplier. Every delivery document is in one place. When a supplier queries an invoice, your answer is three clicks, not three phone calls.

Questions from inbound and procurement leads

Our suppliers use different carriers every week. Can they still use the same link?

Yes. The link belongs to the supplier, not the carrier or driver. Whoever shows up at your dock can use it. There is nothing driver-specific to configure.

We have forty stores. Do all of them need separate setups?

No. You can configure one link per supplier that covers all your locations. The reference field captures the store or site. Your finance team filters by location in the portal.

What if a supplier already uses their own POD app?

Podfy does not replace what the supplier uses internally — it gives you a record on your side. Both can coexist. Your portal is independent of the supplier's tooling.

How does this satisfy our GDPR and data residency requirements?

All data is stored in Cloudflare's EU-WEUR region. No data transits outside the EU. We can provide a Data Processing Agreement for your compliance and procurement process.

What does rollout look like for a retailer with multiple DCs?

Typically two to four hours end to end: create slugs per supplier, share links, and your team can start receiving. No hardware, no IT project, no integration required to begin.
