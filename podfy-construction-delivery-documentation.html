On-site delivery flow

  1. Driver arrives on site
  2. Upload link is shared
  3. Documents and photos uploaded
  4. Project team has instant access

The construction delivery reality

Construction sites are not controlled warehouse environments. Delivery documentation is often the weak link.

Many suppliers and drivers

Different companies, different paperwork, different habits.

Temporary and changing sites

Locations change and infrastructure is limited.

Paper gets lost

Delivery notes disappear before they reach the office.

Disputes over deliveries

Missing proof leads to discussions about quantities and timing.

How Podfy works on construction sites

1. Share the upload link

Send or show a Podfy link to the driver or supplier on arrival.

2. Upload documents and photos

Delivery notes, PODs and photos are uploaded directly from the phone.

3. Central project storage

Everything is stored centrally per site or project.

FAQ

Do drivers need to install an app?

No. Uploading works through a simple browser link.

Can multiple sites use Podfy?

Yes. Each project or site can have its own document flow.

Can photos be added as proof?

Yes. Photos can be uploaded alongside documents.

Is it suitable for audits and disputes?

Yes. Documents are stored centrally and can be retrieved later.
