Cookie Policy

PODFY follows a “minimal and transparent” approach to cookies. We currently do not use marketing or tracking cookies on podfy.net or podfy.app. This page explains what we do today, what might change in the future, and how you stay in control.

1. Overview

How PODFY thinks about cookies

Many products default to heavy tracking. PODFY does not. Our goal is to operate with little or no cookies by default, especially for marketing and analytics, and to only introduce additional cookies when they are clearly justified and transparently controlled.

This policy focuses on browser-level cookies and similar storage technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage). For broader information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

2. Today

Cookies we currently use

As of the last update, PODFY does not use analytics, marketing, or third-party tracking cookies on either podfy.net or podfy.app.

2.1 podfy.net (website)

  • No persistent cookies are set by the website itself.
  • No cross-site tracking or behavioural profiling.
  • Traffic is still logged at the server/edge level for security and reliability, but this happens without client cookies.

2.2 podfy.app (operational platform)

The application may rely on strictly necessary technical storage to maintain secure sessions or preferences. Where used, these are:

  • Limited to authentication and user experience.
  • Not used for third-party tracking or advertising.
  • Not shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Delivery portals & email links

Drivers and partners access POD documents via unique, tokenised links. These tokens act as one-time or time-limited identifiers in the URL and are validated on the server. They are not cookies and are not used to track activity across multiple pages or sites.

3. Future

Possible future cookie usage

As PODFY evolves, we may introduce limited cookies to improve the product. If we do, they will be grouped into clear categories:

  • Essential cookies – required for core functionality (for example, secure login, load balancing, CSRF protection). These do not require consent under EU law but will still be documented.
  • Preference cookies – to remember things like language, theme, or default filters inside the app.
  • Analytics cookies – anonymous or pseudonymous metrics about product usage to improve reliability and UX.

What we will not use cookies for:

  • Advertising or retargeting campaigns.
  • Selling or renting identifiable browsing profiles.
  • Tracking users across unrelated third-party sites.

Consent and updates

If we introduce non-essential cookies (for example analytics), you will see:

  • An updated version of this Cookie Policy.
  • A consent banner or control centre on podfy.net.
  • Options to accept, reject, or fine-tune cookie categories.
4. Control

How you can manage cookies

Even though PODFY uses almost no cookies today, you always have control via your browser. Typical locations for cookie settings:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings / Preferences → Privacy

You can:

  • Block third-party cookies (safe for PODFY).
  • Clear cookies and site data at any time.
  • Set exceptions or stricter controls per site.

For rights related to personal data (access, deletion, objection), see our Privacy Policy.

5. Questions

Questions about this policy

If you have questions about cookies, tracking, or privacy at PODFY, contact us at info@podfy.net.
