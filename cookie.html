Cookies & Browser Storage

Cookie Policy

PODFY follows a strict “no unnecessary cookies” principle. We currently do not use marketing or analytics cookies on podfy.net or podfy.app. This policy explains today’s usage, possible future changes, and how you remain in control.

1. Overview

How PODFY approaches cookies

Most SaaS products deploy trackers by default. PODFY intentionally avoids this. We keep the platform cookie-light and privacy-forward. Only storage that is technically essential may be used, and it is never used for advertising or profiling.

2. Current cookie usage

Cookies used today

podfy.net (marketing website)

  • No marketing, tracking, or analytics cookies.
  • No persistent identifiers stored in your browser.

podfy.app (operational platform)

  • Only strictly necessary technical cookies may be used.
  • These support secure authentication and session integrity.
  • No cookies are shared with third parties.

Delivery portals

Drivers receive tokenised links to view PODs. These tokens are not cookies and do not store information in the browser. They are validated server-side and expire naturally.

3. Possible future cookies

What might change later

If PODFY introduces optional features requiring cookies, they will fall into clear categories:

  • Essential: authentication, CSRF protection, load balancing.
  • Preference: theme, default filters, language.
  • Analytics: anonymised product usage metrics (never advertising).

Consent & transparency

If non-essential cookies are introduced, you will receive:

  • A clear consent banner.
  • Category-level opt-in controls.
  • An updated version of this Cookie Policy.
4. Managing cookies

How to control or delete cookies

Modern browsers allow you to manage cookies at any time:

  • Chrome / Edge → Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox → Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari → Preferences → Privacy

You can block third-party cookies, delete stored data, or set exceptions per domain.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy

For questions about cookies, tracking, or privacy, contact us at support@podfy.net.
