Today

Cookies we currently use

As of today, neither podfy.net nor podfy.app set marketing, tracking, or analytics cookies. We intentionally avoid unnecessary identifiers and do not use third-party trackers such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insights, Hotjar, or similar tools.

podfy.net (website)

  • No persistent cookies.
  • No session identifiers stored in the browser.
  • No marketing or behavioural tracking.

podfy.app (operational platform)

  • We may use a technical session cookie if required for authentication, strictly limited to product functionality.
  • Cookies set in the app are:
    • Essential
    • Not used for tracking
    • Not shared with third parties
    • Automatically cleared after logout or expiry

No cross-site tracking is performed between podfy.net and podfy.app, and no commercial identifiers are collected.

Delivery portals & email links

Drivers and partners access POD documents through token-based URLs. These tokens are not cookies and are not stored in the browser. They are one-directional identifiers used to validate access and do not track a user across pages or devices.

Future

Possible future cookie usage

While PODFY currently operates without cookies beyond those essential to authentication, future versions may introduce optional features that rely on cookies. These would require explicit consent under GDPR/ePrivacy.

Potential future categories

  • Analytics cookies – used for aggregated, anonymised product usage insights.
  • Preference cookies – e.g., storing dark/light theme or default filters inside podfy.app.
  • Optional functional cookies – used to enhance user experience, not required for core functionality.

We will not deploy these without:

  • A prior update of this Cookie Policy
  • Clear, prominent disclosure
  • A consent banner or other opt-in mechanism where required

What we will never do

PODFY will not use cookies for:

  • Advertising or retargeting
  • Tracking users across third-party sites
  • Profiling drivers or end-users
Control

How to manage and delete cookies

Even though PODFY uses almost no cookies today, modern browsers give you full control to block, delete, or restrict them. You can usually find these settings under:

  • Browser settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Clear browsing data
  • “Block third-party cookies” (safe to enable for PODFY)

If we introduce optional cookies in the future, podfy.net will provide an in-page preference center allowing you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Fine-tune categories (analytics, preferences, functional)

For privacy and data rights (including access, erasure, and objection), see our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy

If you have any questions about cookies, tracking, or privacy at PODFY, you can contact us at info@podfy.net.
