Cookies & browser storage

Cookie Policy

PODFY follows a strict “no unnecessary cookies” approach. We currently do not use analytics or marketing cookies on podfy.net or podfy.app. This page explains what we use today, what could change in the future, and how you stay in control.

1. Overview

How PODFY thinks about cookies

Many websites default to heavy tracking. PODFY does not. We aim to operate with as few cookies as possible, especially on the marketing side, and to only introduce additional cookies when they are clearly justified, documented, and controllable by you.

This policy focuses on browser-level cookies and similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage. For broader information about how we process personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

2. Current cookie usage

Cookies we currently use

As of the last update, PODFY does not use analytics, marketing, or third-party tracking cookies on either podfy.net or podfy.app.

2.1 podfy.net (website)

  • No persistent cookies are set by the website itself.
  • No cross-site tracking or behavioural profiling is performed.
  • Server and edge logs are collected for security and reliability, but this happens on the backend without client-side tracking cookies.

2.2 podfy.app (operational platform)

The application may rely on strictly necessary technical storage to maintain secure sessions or product preferences. Where used, these are:

  • Limited to core functionality, such as authentication and CSRF protection.
  • Not used for advertising or third-party profiling.
  • Not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Delivery portals & email links

Drivers and partners access POD documents via unique, tokenised links. These tokens are part of the URL and validated on the server. They are not cookies and are not used to track activity across multiple sessions or domains.

3. Possible future cookies

How we might use cookies later

As PODFY evolves, we may introduce limited cookies to improve product quality and usability. If we do, they will be grouped into clear categories:

  • Essential cookies – strictly required for core functions such as secure login, load-balancing, or protecting forms from abuse.
  • Preference cookies – for remembering settings such as language, theme, or default filters.
  • Analytics cookies – for aggregated, product-level usage metrics, not individual profiling.

We will not use cookies for:

  • Advertising or retargeting networks.
  • Tracking users across unrelated third-party sites.
  • Building or selling identifiable browsing profiles.

Consent, banners, and updates

If we ever introduce non-essential cookies (for example analytics on podfy.net), you will see:

  • An updated version of this Cookie Policy.
  • A clear consent banner or control centre on podfy.net.
  • Options to accept, reject, or fine-tune cookie categories in line with EU requirements.
4. Managing cookies

How you can control or delete cookies

Even though PODFY uses very few cookies, modern browsers give you full control. Typical locations:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Settings / Preferences → Privacy.

You can:

  • Block third-party cookies (safe for PODFY).
  • Delete cookies and site data at any time.
  • Set stricter rules per site if you wish.

For rights related to personal data (access, deletion, objection), see our Privacy Policy.

5. Questions & contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy

If you have questions about cookies, tracking, or privacy at PODFY, you can contact us at support@podfy.net.
