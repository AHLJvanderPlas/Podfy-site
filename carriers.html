The Tuesday morning problem

Your dispatcher calls the driver on the subcontractor route. The CMR from last Friday's delivery is not in the portal. The client is chasing the invoice. The driver is three countries away, and the paper was left in the cab, photographed by nobody, filed by nobody.

The €4,200 invoice goes on hold. Finance chases. The driver phones again a week later. The CMR eventually turns up — blurry, photo on WhatsApp, wrong orientation. Someone has to re-key it. The client still disputes the date.

How it fits a carrier network

01 — Dispatch

Send the link with the job

When you assign the route, include the Podfy link in your dispatch message — WhatsApp, SMS, or TMS note. One URL per shipment reference. The driver does not need to register anything.

02 — Delivery

Driver photographs at the gate

They open the link, take a photo of the signed CMR (or upload a PDF), and tap send. GPS coordinates are captured at that moment. Works whether the driver speaks Dutch, Polish, or Romanian.

03 — Office

Stamped PDF in the portal

Your ops team gets an email notification. The document is in the Podfy portal, searchable by reference or date, ready for invoicing within the same working day.

Questions from ops managers

What if the driver has no mobile signal at the delivery point?

The GPS coordinates are recorded the moment the camera fires, not when the upload completes. The file queues and submits automatically when the phone reaches signal. Most routes have coverage at the gate; rural exceptions are handled without data loss.

We use subcontractors who work for several hauliers. Will they end up with our branded link?

The link is job-specific, not driver-specific. You send it per shipment reference. There is nothing to install, store, or reuse. After the upload, the link is consumed.

Our drivers have mixed phones — some very old Androids, some iPhones. Does it work on all of them?

Yes. Podfy is a web page, not an app. It opens in any browser on any phone from 2018 onwards. No download, no iOS or Android version to keep current.

How long are the CMRs stored?

Standard plans include retention from one to five years depending on the tier. A seven-year audit-ready archive option is available. All data is held in Cloudflare's EU-WEUR region to satisfy Dutch and Belgian data residency requirements.

Can a driver submit multiple photos for a single delivery?

Yes. Multi-file uploads are supported — useful when a delivery involves a CMR plus a pallet photo plus a damage note. All files are grouped under the same reference in the portal.
