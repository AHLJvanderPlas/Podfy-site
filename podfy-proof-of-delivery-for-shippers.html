FAQ

Can Podfy help us collect proof of delivery from carriers delivering on our behalf?

Yes. Podfy helps you collect PODs and delivery documents from any carrier using a simple upload link. Drivers and carriers can upload the required documents without installing an app, which makes adoption easier across multiple partners and different workflows. This gives your team consistent access to delivery proof, even when carriers change.

Do carriers or drivers need to install an app?

No. Podfy works through a browser link sent via SMS, WhatsApp, or email. Drivers can upload documents from any phone without installs, logins, or training. This is especially useful when you work with subcontractors or ad-hoc carriers.

Which documents can we receive and store as proof?

Podfy supports PODs, delivery notes, CMRs, packing slips, and photos. Photos are often used to document delivery condition, damage, or drop-off context. Keeping all proof together helps customer service, billing, and claims handling.

How does Podfy help us invoice customers faster?

Delivery proof is uploaded immediately and stored centrally. Your team does not need to chase carriers, drivers, or email attachments before invoicing. This reduces delays between delivery completion and customer billing and helps avoid disputes caused by missing PODs.

How does Podfy help with claims and disputes?

Podfy keeps delivery documents and photos centralized and searchable. When a customer disputes a delivery, questions timing, or reports damage, your team can retrieve proof quickly and respond with clear documentation instead of assumptions.

How does Podfy handle security and GDPR?

Podfy is designed with GDPR compliance in mind. Documents are stored securely and access is controlled by your organization, ensuring delivery proof is available only to authorized users.
