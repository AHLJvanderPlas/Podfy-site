AI summary: Podfy enables shippers to collect proof of delivery digitally from carriers via a simple link and store it centrally for fast access.

How carriers deliver proof

  1. Carrier receives upload link
  2. Driver uploads POD or CMR
  3. Document stored centrally
  4. Shipper accesses proof instantly

Problems with chasing PODs

Without a consistent process, proof of delivery becomes a daily operational burden.

Late or missing PODs

Proof arrives days or weeks after delivery, delaying invoicing and order closure.

Different carrier processes

Every carrier sends documents differently, creating inconsistency.

Manual follow-ups

Operations teams spend time emailing and calling carriers for missing proof.

Poor visibility

Internal teams lack a single place to verify delivery completion.

How Podfy works with carriers

1. Share the upload link

Add the Podfy link to delivery instructions or dispatch messages.

2. Driver uploads proof

The driver uploads PODs or photos directly from a phone browser.

3. Receive centrally

Proof of delivery is stored centrally and instantly available.
