About PODFY

Built for the driver on any phone.
The office on any screen.

PODFY is a proof of delivery platform for European carriers, 3PLs, and shippers. GPS-stamped POD without a driver app. No account. No training. EU-hosted.

What we do

PODFY captures proof of delivery at the moment of handover. The driver opens a link on any phone, photographs the signed CMR or delivery note, and the GPS-stamped record appears in the operations portal within seconds. No app. No account. No training.

The record contains GPS coordinates captured at the moment the camera fires, not at upload time. It cannot be retroactively altered. It is stored in Cloudflare EU-WEUR, meaning no data leaves the European Union.

Why we built it

Subcontractors don't install apps. Employed drivers won't use three different systems. Paper CMRs arrive days late, or not at all. Shippers increasingly require GPS-stamped digital POD within hours of delivery, while carriers are stuck managing a mix of employed staff, rotating subcontractors, and one-off spot carriers.

We built PODFY so that any carrier, on any phone, can provide GPS-stamped delivery proof in under 30 seconds, without requiring installation, registration, or training on either side of the handover.
