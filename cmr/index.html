FAQ

Questions about CMR and delivery proof

Does the driver need an app or an account?

No. The driver opens a link or scans a QR card, takes a photo and sends it from the browser. Nothing to install, no login.

Does it work for subcontractors?

Yes. Add the reference to your PODFY link or print a QR card from the portal. Subcontracted drivers send their documents the same way, without an account.

Is a photo of the signed CMR enough?

PODFY stores the photo or scan of the signed CMR as a stamped PDF with time, reference and location where available. It gives you a searchable copy for invoicing and disputes; it does not change the legal role of the paper CMR.

What is the difference with e-CMR?

An e-CMR replaces the paper consignment note under the e-CMR protocol. PODFY works with the signed CMR you already use, and with the CMR generator you can also create CMRs in the portal. That makes it a fit for mixed fleets and subcontractors.

Can documents go into our TMS?

Yes. Every record is sent by e-mail and can be sent by webhook to your TMS or ERP. With the open API you fetch records from your own systems, and you can export per period for invoicing.

How long are documents kept?

As long as your plan specifies: from 14 days on Basic up to 7 years on Enterprise. Longer retention is available as an add-on.

What does it cost?

You pay per document, from €0.10 per proof of delivery. No seat fees and no setup costs. The CMR generator is €0.35 per CMR.
