CMR questions

What is a CMR consignment note?

A CMR consignment note is the internationally standardised freight document required for cross-border road transport under the Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road (CMR, Geneva 1956). It records sender, consignee, carrier, goods, and conditions of carriage.

What is the difference between CMR and AVC?

CMR is the international convention form used across Europe and beyond. AVC (Algemene Vervoerscondities 2002) is the Dutch domestic variant with its own standard terms. Podfy supports both modes, switch between CMR and AVC with a single toggle in the form.

How is the CMR add-on priced?

The CMR add-on is priced at €0.35 per CMR generated. This is separate from your POD pricing, generating a CMR does not count as a processed POD. The add-on is available on Advanced and Pro plans.

Do drivers or recipients need to install anything?

No. Podfy generates the CMR PDF in the portal. The document is printed and signed physically, or signed on-screen where accepted. No app installation is required for any party.

How long are CMR records stored?

CMR records are stored for the duration of your plan's retention period. All metadata, the structured form data, and the generated PDF are retained and searchable from the portal. Export is available at any time.

Can I use CMR without also using Podfy for POD?

The CMR generator is an add-on that works alongside your Podfy POD subscription. It is not available as a standalone product. Contact us if you want to discuss your specific setup.
