FAQ

Do people uploading documents need to install an app?

No. Podfy works through a simple browser link sent via SMS, WhatsApp, or email. People can upload documents immediately without installing an app, creating an account, or receiving training. This makes adoption easy, even with external partners.

What documents can we digitize with Podfy?

Podfy can digitize delivery and logistics documents such as PODs, delivery notes, CMRs, packing slips, and photos. Many teams also upload photos to document packaging condition, damage, or delivery context. Everything is stored together in one central archive.

How does Podfy replace scanning and email workflows?

Instead of scanning paperwork and emailing attachments around, Podfy collects files directly through an upload link. Documents go straight into a central, searchable archive. That reduces manual handling and prevents files from getting lost across inboxes and shared drives.

Is Podfy suitable for audits and disputes?

Yes. Podfy keeps documents stored centrally so teams can retrieve proof quickly when something is questioned. This helps with delivery disputes, claims, and audit requests without digging through email threads or paper folders.

How does Podfy handle security and GDPR?

Podfy is designed with GDPR compliance in mind. Documents are stored securely and access is controlled by your organization, ensuring sensitive information remains protected and available only to authorized users.

Does Podfy replace a full document management system?

No. Podfy is a simple, lightweight way to digitize and centralize delivery-related documents without implementing a complex DMS. It complements your existing tools by making document collection fast, consistent, and easy for everyone involved.
