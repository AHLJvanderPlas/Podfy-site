Problems with paper-based workflows

Paper processes slow teams down and create unnecessary risk.

Paper gets lost

Delivery documents disappear before they are scanned or filed.

Scanning creates delays

Documents pile up waiting to be scanned, renamed, and stored.

Email attachments scatter files

PDFs end up across inboxes and folders with no clear structure.

Hard to find later

When a dispute or audit appears, finding the right document takes too long.

How Podfy digitizes documents

Podfy removes friction at the very first step: getting documents into a digital system.

1. Share a link

Send an upload link via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or include it in instructions.

2. Upload instead of scan

Documents are photographed or uploaded directly from a phone or computer.

3. Central digital storage

Files are stored in one place, searchable and available to all relevant teams.

FAQ

No. Podfy is a simple first step to digitize delivery documents, not a complex DMS.

Do users need training?

No. Uploading via a link is intuitive and requires no onboarding.

Can this work alongside existing systems?

Yes. Podfy complements existing tools by handling document intake and storage.
