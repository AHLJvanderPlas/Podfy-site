How it works

01

Driver opens link

Send a URL by WhatsApp, SMS, or email. Any phone, any carrier. No account, no download, no training — if your driver is a subcontractor with a four-year-old Android, Podfy still works.

02

Snaps the CMR

Photo, PDF, or scan. GPS stamp and timestamp are added automatically. The reference is logged against your account. File goes to EU storage immediately.

03

Office gets stamped PDF

Email notification fires in seconds. The document is searchable in the portal, ready for invoicing, dispute resolution, or audit — without opening a ticket.

What you get

What every Podfy record contains

The document

The original uploaded file — signed CMR, delivery note, or photo. Stored as-is in EU-WEUR. Retrievable on demand for 7 years.

GPS coordinates

Latitude and longitude captured the moment the camera fires — not at upload. Server-stored, tamper-evident. Confirms the correct delivery location.

Timestamp

Exact date and time logged server-side. Cannot be retroactively altered. Establishes when the delivery happened — not when the paperwork arrived.

Reference linkage

The PO number, CMR reference, or shipment ID links the record to your invoice. Every delivery retrievable by the identifier your finance team uses.
