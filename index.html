No-app uploads

Drivers, suppliers, and subcontractors upload PODs, CMRs, delivery notes, or photos via a simple link. No app, login, or training required.

Works in real environments

Designed for construction sites, stores, warehouses, facilities, and low-tech environments where controlled workflows are unrealistic.

Central delivery record

All delivery documents are stored centrally, searchable, timestamped, and available for operations, finance, audits, and disputes.

Standard across partners

One neutral process for carriers, 3PLs, suppliers, and stores—without forcing technology adoption on external partners.
