FAQ

Questions about PODFY

What is PODFY?

PODFY is a European platform that collects proof and reports from the field without an app. It has two solutions: CMR and delivery proof for transport and logistics, and QR Reporting for machines and assets.

What is the difference between CMR and delivery proof and QR Reporting?

CMR and delivery proof follows shipments: drivers send the signed CMR or delivery note per shipment reference. QR Reporting follows assets: a permanent QR code on each machine lets anyone report a problem with that asset.

Can we use both?

Yes. Both run on the same platform with the same portal, branding, notifications and webhooks, so one account can collect delivery proof and equipment reports.

Does anyone need to install an app?

No. Drivers and people reporting a problem use the browser on their own phone, without an app or an account.

Where is the data stored?

Documents and reports are stored in the EU. E-mail delivery uses the subprocessors listed on our Trust page.

What does it cost?

CMR and delivery proof is priced per document, from €0.10. QR Reporting is priced per project, based on the number of assets and reports.
