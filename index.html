FAQ

Do I need to install an app or create accounts?

No. Just open the link and upload. It works on any phone or computer.

How fast can we start?

You can try it in minutes with our demo. A branded link can be set up quickly for your team.

Can we add our branding?

Yes. Your upload page can show your logo, colors, and contact email.

Can drivers add a shipment reference?

Yes. The branded plan supports capturing a shipment reference with the upload.

How long are files kept?

With the Portal option, PODs are stored for one year — matching the CMR convention period.

Do you integrate with our systems?

For many teams, no integration is needed. If you want automation, we offer custom webhooks or APIs on request.
