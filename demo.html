The full loop in four steps

01 — Driver side

Open the driver link on your phone

Click the button above (or scan the QR below on a second device). You will see the upload page a driver sees — your brand, one button, no login. Photograph any document: a delivery note, this page, your hand.

02 — Submit

Tap send

GPS coordinates are captured the moment you tap. Enter a reference number if you like, or leave it blank for the demo. The upload completes in seconds on a standard 4G connection.

03 — Office side

Watch it arrive in the portal

Open the delivery portal link above in a separate tab (or on a desktop). Your upload appears within seconds, with the timestamp, GPS label, and a downloadable stamped PDF.

Try it on a real phone

The upload experience is designed for a driver's phone, not a desktop browser. For the most realistic demo, open the driver link on your mobile device.

On your phone: tap the "Open the driver link" button above. Your phone's camera will open. Photograph anything — the screen in front of you counts.

On a desktop: open the driver link in this browser, allow camera access when prompted, and use your webcam to capture a document.
