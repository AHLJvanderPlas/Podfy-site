The full loop in four steps

01 — Driver side

Open the driver upload form

Type an optional reference number above and tap Open upload. You will see the upload page a driver sees — one button, no login. Photograph any document: a delivery note, this page, your hand.

You'll see: a one-button camera screen branded with the demo slug.

02 — Submit

Tap send

Your location is requested when you tap send — if you grant permission, GPS coordinates are added to the stamp. Enter a reference number if you like, or leave it blank. The upload completes in seconds on a standard 4G connection.

You'll see: an upload-progress line, then a "Sent" confirmation.

03 — Office side

Watch it arrive in the portal

Open the delivery portal in a separate tab. Your upload appears within seconds, with the timestamp, GPS label, and a downloadable stamped PDF.

You'll see: a fresh row in the portal — reference, GPS, timestamp, downloadable PDF.

04 — Driver side

Driver gets their own copy

If you filled in the Copy Email field at upload, a branded PDF confirmation arrives at your address — the same record a real driver receives and can access again at delivery.podfy.net via magic link.

You'll see: a branded confirmation email arrive at the address you entered.
