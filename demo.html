Try it now

The full loop in four steps

01 — Driver side

Open the driver link on your phone

Type an optional reference number and tap Open upload. You will see the upload page a driver sees — your brand, one button, no login. Photograph any document: a delivery note, this page, your hand.

02 — Submit

Tap send

Your location is requested when you tap send — if you grant permission, GPS coordinates are added to the stamp. Enter a reference number if you like, or leave it blank. The upload completes in seconds on a standard 4G connection.

03 — Office side

Watch it arrive in the portal

Open the delivery portal link above in a separate tab (or on a desktop). Your upload appears within seconds, with the timestamp, GPS label, and a downloadable stamped PDF.

04 — Driver side

Driver gets their own copy

If the driver fills in the Copy Email field at upload, they receive a branded PDF confirmation automatically — their personal record of the delivery. For this demo, fill in your own email there to see the full flow. Drivers can also view their history at delivery.podfy.net via magic link.

Try it on a real phone

The upload experience is designed for a driver's phone, not a desktop browser. For the most realistic demo, open the driver link on your mobile device.

On your phone: tap the "Open the driver link" button above. Your phone's camera will open. Photograph anything — the screen in front of you counts.

On a desktop: open the driver link in this browser, allow camera access when prompted, and use your webcam to capture a document.
