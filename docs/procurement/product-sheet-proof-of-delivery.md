## The problem

Proof of delivery arrives late, incomplete or on paper. Drivers and subcontractors photograph a CMR or delivery note and send it by chat or e-mail. Back office staff then rename, file and chase. Invoices wait for the document, and disputes cannot be settled quickly.

## How it works

1. You give each driver or subcontractor a PODFY link, or print a QR card per shipment from the portal.
2. The driver opens it on any phone browser, adds the shipment reference and uploads the photo or file. No app, no account, no login.
3. PODFY creates a branded, time-stamped PDF with the reference and the original file. Location is added from the photo or the phone when available and permitted.
4. The PDF is e-mailed to the people you choose (to, cc, bcc), the driver can receive a copy, and the record is stored and searchable in the portal.

## Security and EU

- Documents and data are stored in the EU (Cloudflare EU jurisdiction). E-mail delivery and payments use the subprocessors on the trust page: podfy.net/trust.
- Encryption in transit and at rest, daily backups, tenant separation, audit-ready records.
- Data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses available on request.
- Retention per plan: Basic 14 days, Starter 120 days, Advanced 1 year, Pro 5 years, Enterprise as agreed (up to 7 years). Longer retention is an add-on.
