Purpose of This Page

The Imprint (“Impressum”) is a legally required disclosure in several European jurisdictions, most notably Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, but also increasingly expected by international auditors.

You use this page for:

  • Corporate transparency and identification for visitors and authorities
  • Legal compliance when serving EU/EEA users
  • Providing a single canonical location for company address and registration numbers
  • Supporting partnerships, procurement checks, and due-diligence processes

Do I need to link this from anywhere?

Yes. Best practice is to link the Imprint in the site footer together with Terms, Privacy Policy, and Security.

This ensures compliance for all visitors regardless of entry point (homepage, pricing pages, portals, etc.).

The Imprint does not replace the Terms, Privacy Policy, or Security documentation. Instead, it complements them by providing the **legal identity** behind all PODFY services.
