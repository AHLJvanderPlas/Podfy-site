3. Regulatory purpose

Why this Imprint exists

The Imprint (“Impressum”) fulfils legal information duties that apply in several European jurisdictions (for example Germany, Austria and Switzerland) and provides a single authoritative source for PODFY’s corporate identity data.

Typical use cases include:

  • Visitors and authorities verifying who operates podfy.net and podfy.app.
  • Procurement and vendor-onboarding processes that require trade registry details.
  • Auditors checking that legal contact information is consistently published.
  • Lawful requests that must be addressed to the responsible entity.

This Imprint complements, but does not replace, the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Security documentation. Those documents describe contractual terms, data protection, and technical security measures in more detail.
