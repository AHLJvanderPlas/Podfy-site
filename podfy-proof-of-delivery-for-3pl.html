The 3PL document challenge

Managing proof of delivery across many carriers creates daily operational friction.

Many carriers, many processes

Each carrier sends documents differently, if they send them at all.

Inconsistent POD formats

Photos, PDFs, scans and emails arrive without structure.

Time lost chasing documents

Impact on billing & service

How Podfy works for 3PLs

1. Send an upload link

Share a Podfy link with carriers, subcontractors or drivers via email, SMS or WhatsApp.

2. Upload without an app

Drivers upload PODs, CMRs or photos directly from their browser.

3. Central document storage

Documents are stored centrally and can be linked to shipments and customers.
