The core difference

A generic form builder gives you one form and one QR code. Whoever scans it has to say which machine they mean, where it stands and what is wrong. Typed asset numbers are missing, misspelled or guessed, and someone on your side has to sort it out before anything can be fixed.

PODFY works the other way round. Every asset gets its own QR code, linked to your asset register. The scan already identifies the asset, so the reporter only says what is wrong. The report arrives with the asset number, the location and the group, and it is added to the history of that asset.

  • One QR code per asset, linked to the asset register
  • Asset number, location and group arrive with every report
  • A history of all reports per asset
  • Routing, status and webhooks, not only an e-mail with answers

When a generic form is enough

A form builder with a QR code is a fine choice in some situations. Be honest about which one you are in.

It starts to hurt when assets multiply, when several teams handle reports, or when someone asks which machine fails most often. That is where an asset register pays off.

  • You have a handful of assets in one place and someone reads every message
  • You want feedback or a survey, not a fault report tied to a specific machine
  • You do not need a history per asset, routing or follow up status
  • A one-off event or a short project where setup time matters more than structure

FAQ

Questions about the comparison

Why not just use a form builder with a QR code?

You can, and for a few assets it works. A generic form does not know which asset was scanned, so the reporter must type it. PODFY gives every asset its own QR code linked to the asset register, so the report arrives with asset number, location and group.

Can we keep our existing categories and questions?

Yes. You set your own categories and fields per form, with labels in English, Dutch, German and French, and decide whether a photo is required, optional or off.

What happens to our existing QR codes and stickers?

Stickers from a generic tool point to that tool's form, so they need to be replaced by PODFY stickers. The new stickers carry your logo and your own text, and the QR codes stay linked to your own asset IDs.

Do reports go to our ticketing or maintenance system?

Yes. Reports can be routed per location or category and sent by webhook to your ticketing or maintenance system. An open API for your own integrations is coming soon.

What does PODFY cost compared with a form tool?

€0.68 per active asset per month, down to €0.27 for fleets above 2,500 assets, with a minimum of €13.50 per month. Nothing is charged per report, per user or per QR code, and retired assets are free. The pilot is free for 6 weeks.

Where is the data stored and can we leave?

Reports and photos are stored in the EU. You can export all your data at any time, and after you leave your QR codes keep working through a redirect for 12 months.
