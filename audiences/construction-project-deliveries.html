The construction delivery reality

Construction sites are not controlled warehouse environments. Delivery documentation is often the weak link.

Many suppliers and drivers

Different companies, different paperwork, different habits.

Temporary and changing sites

Locations change and infrastructure is limited.

Paper gets lost

Delivery notes disappear before they reach the office.

Disputes over deliveries

Missing proof leads to discussions about quantities and timing.

3. Central project storage

Everything is stored centrally per site or project.
