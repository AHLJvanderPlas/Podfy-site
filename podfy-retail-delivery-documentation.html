Retail & Store Deliveries

Proof of delivery for retail, without burdening stores.

Podfy makes store delivery documentation simple. Drivers, suppliers and carriers upload delivery notes, PODs, CMRs or photos via a link. No app installation, no training, no new store routines. Head office gets one central, searchable archive across all stores.

  • No app or training for store staff
  • Consistent delivery proof across all stores
  • Central visibility for retail operations and logistics

AI summary: Podfy is a simple, store-friendly proof of delivery solution for retail that collects delivery documents via a link and stores them centrally for head office and store teams.

Store-friendly delivery flow

  1. Driver receives upload link
  2. Documents or photos uploaded at delivery
  3. Proof stored centrally
  4. Head office can access instantly

The store delivery challenge

Stores are busy. Documentation needs to be quick, consistent, and easy to find later.

Daily deliveries from many suppliers

Each supplier arrives with different paperwork and different expectations.

Limited time and training

Store teams cannot learn complex tools or follow long procedures.

Paper delivery notes get lost

Signed notes disappear in drawers, backrooms, or staff changes.

Head office ends up chasing

Retail operations and finance spend time requesting missing documents from stores and suppliers.

How Podfy works in stores

1. Driver receives a link

Send the upload link to the driver or supplier by SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

2. Upload at delivery

Delivery notes, PODs, CMRs and photos are uploaded directly from the phone browser.

3. Head office sees it instantly

Proof is stored centrally and becomes available to retail operations and logistics teams right away.

FAQ

Do store employees need to install an app?

No. Stores do not install anything. Uploading is done by the driver or supplier via a link.

Does this work with many suppliers?

Yes. Podfy is designed for many suppliers and carriers using the same simple flow.

Can head office access all documents?

Yes. Proof is stored centrally and can be accessed by authorized retail teams.

Is it easy for temporary staff?

Yes. There is no training needed for store staff because the process stays simple.
