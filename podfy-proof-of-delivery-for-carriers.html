Carrier problems today

If you run trucks, you know the issue is rarely “software”. It’s paperwork flow, driver adoption, and finding the right proof when someone calls.

Drivers don’t want another app

Installs, logins, updates, permissions. Adoption drops and the office ends up chasing documents again.

PODs arrive late (or never)

Paper PODs get lost in the cab, show up days later, or never show up at all. Claims and disputes follow.

Searching takes too long

Someone asks for a signed CMR and the team checks email threads, shared drives, and personal folders.

Customers expect instant proof

Shippers want delivery confirmation fast. Slow responses look like you’re not in control.

How Podfy works (3 steps)

Podfy is built to be the simplest workflow that still gives your office clean, searchable proof.

Step 1

Send a link

Dispatch sends an upload link by SMS, WhatsApp, or email. No accounts required for drivers.

Step 2

Driver uploads

The driver takes photos or uploads files: PODs, CMRs, delivery notes, damage photos. This is proof of delivery without app installs.

Step 3

Office finds and shares

Documents are stored centrally and searchable. Share proof quickly when customers request it.

FAQ

Quick answers for carriers evaluating proof of delivery software.

Do drivers need to install an app?

No. Drivers upload through a browser link. That’s the point: proof of delivery without app installs.

What can drivers upload?

PODs, CMRs, delivery notes, photos (including damage photos) and any supporting documents.

Is it usable for small carriers?

Yes. Podfy is intentionally a simple POD solution that works from 1 truck up to 100 vehicles.

Can we share proof with customers?

Yes. Office staff can access and share documents quickly when customers request delivery confirmation.

Do we need an IT project?

No. Start with a link-based flow and expand later if you want. Podfy is built for quick setup.
