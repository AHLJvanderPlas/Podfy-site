The facilities delivery challenge

Facilities receive many deliveries every day, often without a clear or consistent record.

Many suppliers and couriers

Different vendors arrive throughout the day with different paperwork.

Handled by reception or security

Deliveries are often accepted by staff with limited time and tools.

Paper notes get lost

Signed delivery slips disappear in desks, folders, or shift changes.

No central overview

Facilities teams lack a single place to check what was delivered and when.

How Podfy works for facilities

1. Share the upload link

Send or show a Podfy link to the supplier or courier on arrival.

2. Upload note or photo

Delivery notes or photos are uploaded directly from the phone browser.

3. Stored centrally per site

Documents are stored in a central delivery log per location.

FAQ

No. They only open a link and upload the document or photo.

Can deliveries be separated by site?

Yes. Documents can be organized per building or location.

Can reception staff use this easily?

Yes. The process is designed for quick handover situations.

Is it suitable for audits or incidents?

Yes. Delivery records can be retrieved later when needed.
