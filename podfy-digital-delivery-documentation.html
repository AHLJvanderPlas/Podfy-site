FAQ

Do couriers, suppliers, or contractors need to install an app?

No. Podfy works through a simple browser link sent via SMS, WhatsApp, or email. Couriers, suppliers, and contractors can upload delivery documents or photos immediately without installing an app, creating an account, or receiving training. This is ideal for facilities with many external visitors.

What documents can we capture with Podfy?

Podfy supports delivery notes, PODs, and photos. Facilities teams often use photos to record where items were dropped off, document packaging condition, or confirm delivery at reception or security. All files are stored together per delivery.

Can we use Podfy across multiple sites or buildings?

Yes. Podfy can be used across multiple sites or buildings. Each location can have its own delivery records, while your organization keeps a central overview across all facilities.

Is Podfy easy for reception or security staff to use?

Yes. Podfy is designed for simplicity. Sharing a link and uploading a document or photo takes seconds, making it easy for reception desks, security teams, or on-site staff with limited time.

How does Podfy help with audits, incidents, and vendor disputes?

Podfy creates a searchable delivery record for each site. When an item is missing, disputed, or investigated, delivery proof can be retrieved quickly without searching through emails or paper folders.

How does Podfy handle security and GDPR?

Podfy is designed with GDPR compliance in mind. Delivery documents are stored securely and access is controlled by your organization, ensuring sensitive delivery information remains protected and accessible only to authorized users.
