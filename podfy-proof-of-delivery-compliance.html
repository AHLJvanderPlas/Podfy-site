FAQ

How does Podfy handle security and GDPR?

Podfy is designed with GDPR compliance in mind. Delivery documents are stored securely and access is controlled by your organization, so only authorized users can view delivery records. This helps teams digitize proof of delivery while keeping control over sensitive information.

Is Podfy suitable for audits and compliance checks?

Yes. Podfy keeps proof of delivery documents and photos stored centrally and searchable. That means audit requests can be handled quickly without collecting files from multiple inboxes, folders, or people. It also supports internal reviews where traceability and consistency matter.

What proof can we store for disputes and claims?

Podfy supports PODs, delivery notes, CMRs, and photos. Many teams also use photos to document delivery condition, damage, packaging issues, or drop-off context. Storing everything together makes claims handling and dispute resolution faster and clearer.

How quickly can we retrieve delivery proof when it is requested?

Proof is stored centrally so it can be retrieved quickly when a customer, auditor, or internal stakeholder asks for it. This reduces delays in invoicing and avoids time lost searching through emails, shared drives, or paper folders.

Do drivers need to install an app to provide proof?

No. Drivers upload delivery documents via a simple browser link sent through SMS, WhatsApp, or email. Removing app installs and logins reduces friction and improves consistency in proof collection, especially when subcontractors or temporary drivers are involved.

Does Podfy replace our ERP, DMS, or claims tooling?

No. Podfy is a lightweight layer for collecting and storing delivery proof. It complements your existing systems by making document collection fast and consistent, without requiring a complex document management implementation.
