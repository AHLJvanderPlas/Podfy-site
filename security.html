General

Security governance & infrastructure

Security is owned by PODFY’s management team. Day-to-day security decisions are made as if a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Head of Engineering are sitting at the same desk: security is treated as a product requirement, not an afterthought.

Hosting & platform

  • PODFY runs on Cloudflare’s edge platform, including Cloudflare Pages and Workers.
  • File storage (for POD documents and related uploads) is handled via an object store (e.g. Cloudflare R2) with strong durability and encryption at rest.
  • Relational/metadata storage is handled via a managed SQL-style service (e.g. Cloudflare D1) with access limited to our application layer.
  • We keep a clear separation between production and non-production environments.

Transport security

  • All PODFY properties enforce HTTPS; HTTP is redirected to HTTPS.
  • .app TLD, which requires HTTPS by design.
  • We use TLS 1.2+ and support modern cipher suites. HTTP/3/QUIC is enabled on our edge where available.

Edge, network & perimeter

  • Traffic is terminated at Cloudflare’s edge, benefiting from DDoS protection, a managed WAF, bot protections, and rate-limiting capabilities.
  • Where appropriate, we apply additional rules for sensitive routes (upload endpoints, authentication, delivery portals, etc.).
  • No direct SSH/RDP access to production workloads; deployments are pushed via CI/CD.

Secrets & configuration

  • API keys, credentials, and signing secrets are stored using the platform’s encrypted secrets management, not in source control.
  • Configuration is split per environment (staging/production) and shipped through the deployment pipelines.

Secure development lifecycle

  • We favor a minimal, well-understood stack: static front-ends, edge functions, and a small set of maintained libraries (for example, pdf-lib for PDF handling and exifr for EXIF parsing).
  • Changes are managed via version control and pull requests; security-sensitive changes (authentication, upload handling, email and portal flows) are reviewed more strictly.
  • We maintain Content Security Policy (CSP) headers and iterate on them together with other HTTP security headers (HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, etc.).

Logging, monitoring & auditing

  • Edge and application logs capture enough detail for debugging and incident analysis, without storing unnecessary personal data.
  • Key security events (logins, file uploads, file downloads/views in delivery portals) are logged with tenant and request context where possible.
  • Operational logs are restricted to staff on a need-to-know basis.

Compliance posture

  • We design our controls to align with the principles of ISO 27001 and SOC 2, but we do not claim formal certification at this time.
  • PODFY follows GDPR principles for customers in the EU and offers a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) where relevant.
Application

podfy.app – product & data security

podfy.app is the operational interface where customers configure their POD flows, invite users, and manage documents. It is treated as the primary security perimeter.

Tenant isolation & access control

  • PODFY uses per-customer tenant isolation at the data layer. Business entities are scoped to an account, and access is checked on each relevant operation.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) governs who can configure workflows, invite users, or access documents within a tenant.
  • Access to POD documents is further constrained through signed URLs or link tokens which are long, unguessable, and time-limited where appropriate.
  • Administrative access by PODFY staff is limited, audited, and based on the principle of least privilege.

Authentication & sessions

  • All authentication flows run exclusively over HTTPS.
  • Sessions are maintained via secure cookies or tokens, protected with Secure and HttpOnly flags where applicable.
  • We apply sensible session expiry windows and may invalidate sessions early as part of incident response or account changes.

POD documents, uploads & retention

  • POD documents and related artifacts (scans, PDFs, photos) are stored in an encrypted object store; metadata is stored in a relational store.
  • We support EU data residency for production data handling.
  • Customers can define retention windows for POD documents; after expiry, files are scheduled for deletion from primary storage.
  • For upcoming multi-file upload and PDF normalisation, processing follows a pipeline that:
    • accepts a controlled set of MIME types (PDF, image formats used for PODs),
    • normalises them into a branded PDF for consistency,
    • keeps original files only where functionally necessary.

Delivery portals & email-based access

  • PODFY supports delivery portals that allow drivers or partners to view a POD via a link sent by email.
  • These links use unguessable tokens and may be:
    • restricted in time (e.g. valid for a limited number of days),
    • restricted in scope (access to one POD or shipment),
    • logged when opened (IP, timestamp, user-agent where appropriate).
  • We avoid embedding sensitive data directly in URLs; tokens act as references to server-side records.

Operational safeguards

  • Critical operations (e.g. changing retention settings, deleting large sets of PODs) may require confirmation flows to reduce operator error.
  • Uploads are protected by request size limits and application-level validation.
  • Background jobs (e.g. PDF compilation, email sending) run with limited privileges and narrow scopes.
Data residency & backups

Production POD data is stored in EU data centres where the underlying platform supports it. Backups and replicas may be geographically redundant within the region used by the platform provider. We do not move customer data outside the chosen region for product functionality without a clear reason and appropriate safeguards.

Website

podfy.net – website, forms & email flows

podfy.net is our public website, including marketing pages, pricing, and contact/demo forms. It is kept intentionally simple and separated from the application runtime.

Architecture

  • The site is deployed as a static front-end on Cloudflare Pages, with dynamic behaviour handled via Workers APIs.
  • There is no direct database access from the browser; form submissions are processed by backend Workers and stored in managed services.

Forms & anti-abuse

  • Forms (e.g. “request demo”, contact, pricing configuration) are protected with Cloudflare Turnstile or comparable anti-bot mechanisms.
  • Input is validated server-side before being processed or written to storage.
  • We minimise the data collected on the marketing site to what is needed for contact and follow-up.

Email templates, auto-replies & portals

  • Outgoing emails (auto-replies, portal invites, driver emails) are rendered using a templating system that separates:
    • static branding (logos, colours, general layout), and
    • dynamic data (links, contact details, shipment/POD references).
  • Links embedded in emails to portals or app features use the same tokenised approach described above and are HTTPS-only.
  • We avoid putting sensitive information in subject lines.

Cookies, tracking & analytics

  • PODFY aims for a low-cookie, privacy-conscious approach on podfy.net.
  • Where analytics are used, they are configured to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information; details are described in our Privacy Policy.
Isolation between podfy.net and podfy.app

The marketing site and the operational app are treated as separate surfaces: sessions are not shared, databases are distinct, and bugs in one do not automatically imply compromise of the other.

Data protection

Data protection, privacy & GDPR

PODFY acts as a data processor for POD documents and related data stored in podfy.app, and as a data controller for website and commercial contact data on podfy.net.

Principles

  • Data minimisation: we collect only the data needed to provide the service or respond to a request.
  • Purpose limitation: POD data is used only for POD-related processing (storage, retrieval, sharing with authorised parties, audit and reporting).
  • Access limitation: internal access to customer data is limited to staff who need it for support, operations, or legal reasons.

Data subject rights & DPA

  • For EU customers, we support GDPR rights such as access, rectification, and deletion, typically coordinated through the customer (data controller).
  • We offer a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) including standard contractual clauses where relevant.

Sub-processors

  • PODFY relies on a small number of infrastructure providers (for example, Cloudflare for edge, storage, and databases) and email delivery providers.
  • We aim to keep our list of sub-processors short and subject to data-processing terms aligned with GDPR requirements.

More details on privacy, legal bases and data subject rights can be found in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Incidents

Incident response & vulnerability management

We treat security incidents as first-class events. The same people shipping features are expected to understand how to contain and remediate issues when they occur.

Incident response process

  • Detection: via monitoring alerts, internal reporting, or reports from customers/researchers.
  • Triage: incidents are quickly classified (Critical / High / Medium / Low) based on impact and likelihood.
  • Containment: we may revoke tokens, rotate keys, deploy hotfixes, or temporarily disable affected features.
  • Eradication & recovery: fix the root cause, verify integrity, restore from backups where required.
  • Post-incident review: document what happened, what we learned, and which controls will be strengthened.

Vulnerability remediation targets

  • Critical: target fix or effective mitigation within 7 days.
  • High: target fix within 30 days.
  • Medium: target fix within 90 days.
  • Low: target fix within 180 days.
Vulnerability reporting & responsible disclosure

If you believe you have found a security issue in podfy.app or podfy.net, please email info@podfy.net with details.

We aim to:

  • Acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
  • Provide initial triage within 7 business days.
  • Work with you on remediation according to the severity targets above.

For scope, rules of engagement, safe-harbour language and credit policy, see:

  • PODFY Security Policy (SECURITY.md)
  • /.well-known/security.txt

We ask researchers to avoid actions that could harm availability, integrity, or confidentiality for other customers, such as large-scale automated scanning against production, attempting to access other tenants’ data, or using real third-party identities without consent.

Contact for authorities & customers

  • General security contact: support@podfy.net.
  • Existing customers can also contact us through their usual account manager or support channel, referencing “Security / Incident”.
