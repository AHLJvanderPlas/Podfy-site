Step 1 · PODFY.app basics

Open PODFY.app and capture a POD

Start with the default app at podfy.app or with a branded URL such as podfy.app/furore.

  • Photo / document upload – upload or capture a photo of the signed CMR, delivery note or pallet photo.
  • Reference – depending on the link, a reference field can be added to tag your shipment (see the demo ship example below).
  • Clean toggle – mark the delivery as clean (no issues) or leave it unset when there are damages or remarks.
  • Copy email – the email address where a copy of the POD is sent. This is also your key for the free-tier demo.
No login required for PODFY.app Works from any modern browser
Step 2 · Fields explained

GPS, clean flag and the copy email field

GPS

  • PODFY.app can store location data (when allowed by the device/browser) as part of the transaction.
  • This helps to verify where a POD was captured and can be used for audits and quality checks.

Clean flag

  • Use the Clean option to confirm the delivery was completed without issues.
  • If there is damage, delay or a discrepancy, leave it unset and describe the situation in the remarks.

Copy email field

  • This is the email address that receives a copy of the POD after upload.
  • In the free-tier demo, this address is also used as the identity in the delivery portal.
  • For testing, enter an address you can access yourself (for example, your own work email).
GPS = where Clean flag = quality Copy email = portal identity
Step 3 · Free tier demo

Use the copy email as your free-tier identity

The free-tier demo is built around the Copy email field. Every time you upload a POD and fill in the same email address, all those deliveries are linked together behind the scenes.

  • Choose one test address (for example driver.demo@example.com).
  • Enter this address in the Copy email field for every test POD you upload.
  • You will receive a POD copy per delivery and can later use the same email to log in to the delivery portal.

This allows you to practice with real flows (drivers, planners, recipients) without configuring users or creating accounts.

One email = one demo identity Ideal for training and pilots
Step 4 · Delivery portal

Sign in with a magic link and view your PODs

The delivery portal is available at delivery.podfy.net. There are no passwords to remember – access is granted via a one-time magic link.

How it works

  • Open delivery.podfy.net.
  • Enter the same email as used in the Copy email field.
  • Click “Send magic link”.
  • Check your inbox and click the sign-in link.

What you see after login

  • A table with all deliveries linked to your email for the last 60 days.
  • A search field above the table that filters across the main columns (party, PODFY ID, Reference, date, clean/issue status and remarks) while you type.
  • Columns for party (slug), PODFY ID, Reference, date, time, clean/issue status, remarks and a POD button.
  • Click “View POD” to open the image or PDF in a modal window and download it if needed.

Search bar

  • Start typing any part of a PODFY ID, customer name, reference or remark to reduce the list in real time.
  • Use it during calls with customers to instantly locate a single shipment within all deliveries for that email.

Reference column

  • Shows the shipment or order reference that was captured in PODFY.app, for example from a URL like podfy.app/furore/demo-shipment123.
  • If a POD was created without a reference, the column stays empty – but those rows are still included in search.
  • The search bar can be used to filter directly on a specific reference value.
Magic link only – no password Full-table search Reference-aware filtering Last 60 days included in free tier
Step 5 · Branded demo

Use a branded demo page (Furore example)

In a branded demo tier, your PODFY.app link is customised to your brand. The following links show how this works using a fictional example customer Furore.

Base branded page

https://podfy.app/furore

Opening this link shows a PODFY page branded for Furore – layout, colors and logo are aligned with the customer.

Reference example with shipment ID

https://podfy.app/furore/demo-shipment123

Adding /demo-shipment123 to the URL creates a pre-filled Reference field inside the app. The text after /furore/ becomes the reference value linked to that POD.

  • The reference can be any shipment ID, order number or internal code.
  • It is stored alongside the POD and visible in your reporting and search.
  • This makes it easy for drivers to work with structured references without extra typing.

Step 6 · Retention & upgrades

Understand the 60-day limit and upgrade options

60-day free history

  • The delivery portal shows the last 60 days of deliveries linked to your email.
  • Older data is still stored safely within PODFY but not visible in the free-tier portal.

Paid retention tiers

  • Customer plans can extend retention from 1 year up to 7 years.
  • Retention is configured per customer, aligned with compliance and audit needs.
Free: 60 days visible Paid: 1–7 years visibility
