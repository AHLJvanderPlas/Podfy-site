Step 1 · PODFY.app basics

Open PODFY.app and capture a POD

Start with podfy.app or a branded URL such as podfy.app/furore.

  • Photo / document upload – capture a signed CMR, delivery note or pallet photo.
  • Reference – optionally pre-filled via the URL, used to tag your shipment.
  • Clean toggle – mark the delivery as clean when there are no issues.
  • Copy email – the address that receives the POD copy and unlocks the portal.
No login required for PODFY.app Works from any modern browser
Step 2 · Fields explained

GPS, clean flag and the copy email field

GPS

  • Location is stored when the device and browser permit it.
  • Useful for audits and “where was this POD captured?” questions.

Clean flag

  • Use Clean for fully OK deliveries.
  • Leave it unset and add a remark when there are damages or delays.

Copy email

  • Receives the POD copy as soon as the driver submits.
  • In the free tier, it also acts as your identity in the delivery portal.
GPS = where Clean flag = quality Copy email = portal identity
Step 3 · Free tier identity

Use the copy email as your demo identity

Choose one test address – for example driver.demo@example.com – and use it in the Copy email field for every test POD.

  • All those deliveries are linked behind the scenes to that email.
  • You can share the same address with colleagues during a pilot.
  • Every POD shows up under this identity in the delivery portal.
One email = one demo identity Great for training and pilots
Step 4 · Delivery portal

Sign in with a magic link and view your PODs

Go to delivery.podfy.net and enter the same email you used as copy address.

  • Request a magic link – no password required.
  • Click the link in your inbox to open the portal.

What you will see

  • A list of all deliveries for the last 60 days for that email.
  • A search box that filters across party, PODFY ID, reference, and remarks.
  • A reference column when your URL included a shipment ID or order number.
Magic link only – no passwords Full table search Reference-aware filtering

Step 6 · Retention & plans

Understand the 60-day limit and upgrades

Free tier

  • Portal shows the last 60 days of PODs for your email.
  • Older data is still stored but not exposed in this demo portal.

Paid tiers

  • Retention can be extended from 1 year up to 7 years.
  • Access and retention are configured per customer and role.
Step 7 · From demo to production

Upgrade to a branded demo or full PODFY deployment

Once you are comfortable with the free-tier demo and the delivery portal, you can upgrade to a branded demo or full production setup. In a branded tier you receive:

  • Your own branded PODFY.app link (for example podfy.app/yourbrand).
  • Branded delivery pages and emails for your customers and carriers.
  • Configured retention (1–7 years) and access control per role.
  • Support for multiple slugs (customers) in one unified platform.
