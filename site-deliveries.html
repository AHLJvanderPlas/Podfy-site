The two-month-later problem

The concrete supplier's invoice arrives. It says twelve loads were delivered. Your site log says eleven. The supplier says twelve. Two months have passed. The driver is on a different project. The delivery sheet was signed and left in the cab.

Construction deliveries are the hardest POD to collect correctly. Gates change, site managers move between plots, and drivers are under time pressure. A paper signature at the gatehouse does not prove which gate, which quantity, or which time — and it does not survive a wet site visit.

Podfy puts a GPS camera in every driver's pocket. When they arrive, they open the link, photograph the delivery note or the pallet, and tap send. The GPS coordinates, timestamp, and reference are locked in before the truck moves.

  • GPS coordinates captured at time of delivery — not upload
  • Photo evidence of quantity and condition at the gate
  • Searchable by site reference, date, or supplier
  • Works without on-site Wi-Fi or printer
  • No app on site devices — any phone, any browser
  • Dispute resolution from portal in minutes

How it works on a project site

01 — Per gate

A link per site or gate

You configure a Podfy link for the project — one per site or one per gate if you track deliveries separately. Share it with your suppliers and put it in your delivery instructions. No app, no QR code reader needed.

02 — At arrival

Driver photographs the load

When the truck arrives, the driver opens the link and takes a photo of the delivery note and the load if needed. GPS is stamped automatically. They add the reference number and tap send — before they back away from the gate.

03 — Project office

Site manager reviews in the portal

Every delivery for the project is searchable by date, reference, or supplier. When an invoice arrives, you compare it to the portal in seconds. GPS coordinates confirm the delivery location, not just the date.

Questions from site and project managers

Our site has limited 4G coverage. Will uploads fail?

GPS coordinates and the photo are captured the moment the driver taps send. If there is no signal, the upload queues and submits as soon as the phone reaches coverage — often before the driver leaves site boundaries. The timestamp remains the original capture time.

We have multiple gates on a large site. Can we track which gate received what?

Yes. You can configure separate Podfy links per gate, each with a different reference prefix. Or use one link and require drivers to enter a gate code in the reference field. GPS coordinates also differentiate gate locations in the portal.

Our suppliers' drivers sometimes speak only German or Polish. Will they manage?

The upload page has one button and one photo prompt. No language is required to use it. The reference field is optional — a driver who speaks no Dutch can still submit a valid GPS-stamped photo proof.

Can we attach multiple photos — for example, the delivery note and a photo of the pallet condition?

Yes. Multi-file uploads are supported. All files are grouped under the same reference in the portal so the context is never lost.

We need records for seven years for Belastingdienst audits. Is that covered?

Yes. A seven-year audit-ready archive option is available. All records are stored in Cloudflare's EU-WEUR region and can be exported as PDFs or via API if needed for audit packages.
