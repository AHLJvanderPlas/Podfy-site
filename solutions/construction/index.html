Why construction deliveries are high-risk

Construction site deliveries are among the most disputed in logistics. No fixed reception desk. Multiple subcontractors on site. Goods moved before anyone takes inventory. Deliveries outside working hours to unmanned gates.

When a shortage or damage claim arrives — often days after delivery — no one can say with certainty what was delivered, where it was placed, or who was present. The carrier and the site manager have conflicting accounts, and there is no contemporaneous evidence.

GPS-stamped photo POD at the moment of drop solves this completely. The photo shows the goods in place at the delivery point. The GPS confirms the correct site. The timestamp proves when. Before the goods are moved, before the morning shift starts, the record exists.

  • Works for unmanned and out-of-hours drops
  • GPS confirms correct site and delivery zone
  • Photo shows goods condition and quantity at placement
  • No app — drivers use any phone, no account needed
  • Issue codes for shortage / damage / refusal at time of delivery
  • Export by project reference for contract audits
