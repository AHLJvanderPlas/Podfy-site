"The coffee machine is broken" is not a useful ticket.

Coffee machine service providers look after hundreds of machines in offices they do not run. Facility managers get vague messages, pass them on, and the technician still has to find out which machine it is and what is actually wrong.

With PODFY QR Reporting every machine carries its own QR code. An employee scans it, picks the problem and sends it. The service provider or facility team receives a report that names the machine and the location, often with a photo of the display.

PODFY does not diagnose the machine or plan maintenance. It makes sure the report reaches the right people, complete.

  • One QR code per machine, location included
  • Categories for water, beans, cleaning and leaks
  • Photo of the error screen
  • Reports to the service provider, facilities or both
  • Your branding or your customer's on the form
  • History per machine and per building
A leak near electrical equipment is a safety issue. The sticker can point to the local facility number for urgent situations; the report is for follow-up.

Getting started

Your asset list in, stickers out

  1. Download the template and fill in your asset numbers, names and locations, or export them from your own system.
  2. Upload it to PODFY. Every asset number becomes the reference behind its own QR code.
  3. Print the stickers. One per asset, with your logo and your own text, such as "Coffee machine issue? Scan me".
  4. Stick and go. Reports arrive with the asset already identified.

FAQ

Questions about coffee machines

Can reports go to both the service provider and our facility team?

Yes. Each report can be e-mailed to several addresses, for example the service provider's desk and your own facilities mailbox.

Can the form carry our customer's branding?

Yes. As with PODFY's delivery links, the reporting form shows the logo and colours you choose.

Is a photo required?

You decide. Per QR form you choose whether a photo is required, optional or switched off.

Can reports go straight into our ticketing or maintenance system?

Yes. Reports are e-mailed to the addresses you choose and can be sent to your ticketing or maintenance system with a webhook. An open API for your own integrations is coming.
