Lockers are unattended. Problems are not.

Parcel locker networks grow fast and sit in stations, supermarkets and residential areas. When a door jams or a screen fails, the recipient calls the carrier's support line, which cannot see the locker either, and the field technician gets a vague ticket.

PODFY QR Reporting puts a QR code on every locker unit, and if you want on every compartment. Recipients and couriers report what is wrong in seconds. Your operations team sees the locker, the compartment and the time, and can send the right technician.

PODFY routes the report. Parcel tracing and customer claims stay in your own systems.

  • QR code per locker or per compartment
  • Categories for doors, screens, codes and damage
  • Reports from recipients and couriers
  • Webhook into your field service tool
  • History per locker location
  • Built on PODFY's logistics platform
PODFY does not trace parcels or settle claims. The sticker can show where recipients go for parcel questions; the report is about the locker.

Getting started

Your asset list in, stickers out

  1. Download the template and fill in your asset numbers, names and locations, or export them from your own system.
  2. Upload it to PODFY. Every asset number becomes the reference behind its own QR code.
  3. Print the stickers. One per asset, with your logo and your own text, such as "Locker problem? Scan to report".
  4. Stick and go. Reports arrive with the asset already identified.

FAQ

Questions about parcel lockers

Can we report per compartment?

Yes. One code per locker unit is enough for most networks, but compartments can have their own code so the report says exactly which door.

Can couriers use the same QR code?

Yes. Anyone can scan it; you can add a category only couriers use, such as "locker full".

Do the people reporting need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the form opens in the browser. No app, no login, no account.

Can reports go straight into our ticketing or maintenance system?

Yes. Reports are e-mailed to the addresses you choose and can be sent to your ticketing or maintenance system with a webhook. An open API for your own integrations is coming.
