Visitors notice. Cleaners find out at the next round.

Facility service companies, stations, offices, venues and events clean on a schedule. Between rounds, an empty dispenser or a blocked toilet stays that way, and the complaint reaches the client before it reaches the cleaner.

PODFY QR Reporting puts a QR code in every restroom. Visitors pick what needs attention and send it. Your cleaning team gets a report with the exact restroom and time, and the history shows which locations need more frequent rounds.

It works for any space where visitors can tell you more than a schedule can.

  • QR code per restroom or per area
  • Categories for cleaning, supplies, blockages and leaks
  • Reports to the cleaning team on duty
  • History per location to plan rounds
  • Your client's branding on the form
  • No app, works for every visitor
A leak or flooding near electrical installations goes to facilities or security directly. The report is for cleaning and follow-up.

Getting started

Your asset list in, stickers out

  1. Download the template and fill in your asset numbers, names and locations, or export them from your own system.
  2. Upload it to PODFY. Every asset number becomes the reference behind its own QR code.
  3. Print the stickers. One per asset, with your logo and your own text, such as "Needs cleaning? Scan to report".
  4. Stick and go. Reports arrive with the asset already identified.

FAQ

Questions about restrooms and cleaning

Can reports go to the cleaner on duty?

Reports go to the addresses you choose, for example the shift supervisor's mailbox, or to your planning system with a webhook.

Can we use our client's branding?

Yes. Each client can have its own logo and colours on the form and the stickers.

Is a photo required?

You decide. Per QR form you choose whether a photo is required, optional or switched off.

What does QR Reporting cost?

Pricing depends on the number of assets and reports. Ask for a proposal and we will set it up for your situation.
