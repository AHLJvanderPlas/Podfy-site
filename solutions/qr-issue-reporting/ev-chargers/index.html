Monitoring sees the charger. Drivers see the problem.

Charge point monitoring tells you whether a charger is online. It does not always tell you that a cable is damaged, that a screen is unreadable in the sun or that a payment card keeps being refused. The driver standing at the charger knows, and usually has no easy way to tell you.

PODFY Report gives every charge point, and if you want every connector, its own QR code. The driver picks what is wrong, can add a photo and sends it. Your team receives a report with the station, the connector and the time, ready to compare with your own monitoring data.

Driver reports complement your monitoring and support line. They do not replace either.

  • Report per charge point or per connector
  • Categories for start, connector, display and payment issues
  • Photo of a damaged cable or screen, optional
  • Reports e-mailed to your service desk or maintenance partner
  • History per charger to spot recurring faults
  • No app for the driver, works on any phone
Not an emergency channel. A burnt connector, sparks or exposed wiring need your emergency procedure and the number on the charger first. A QR scan also does not prove that someone is standing at the charger, so treat reports as information, not as verified events.

FAQ

Questions from ev chargers teams

Does this replace our charge point monitoring?

No. Monitoring tells you whether a charger is online. Driver reports add what monitoring cannot see, such as physical damage or payment problems, so the two work together.

Can we report per connector?

Yes. You can give a whole charge point one QR code, or give every connector its own code so the report already says which one.

Do the people reporting need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the form opens in the browser. No app, no login, no account.

Can reports go straight into our ticketing or maintenance system?

At launch every report is e-mailed to the addresses you choose and kept in the PODFY portal. Direct forwarding to other systems is on the roadmap; tell us which system you use.
