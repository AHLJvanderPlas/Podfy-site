Problems get noticed. They rarely get reported well.

The person standing in front of a broken machine is usually not the person who can fix it. They have to find a phone number on a faded sticker, explain which machine they mean and describe the problem from memory. Most give up. The rest send an e-mail without a machine number.

PODFY Report puts a QR code on every machine. Scanning it opens a short form that already knows the machine and its location. The reporter only says what is wrong. Your team receives a structured report it can act on, and every report stays in a history per machine.

It is built on the platform that already handles proof of delivery for logistics teams: no app for the reporter, your own branding, EU storage and a searchable portal.

  • No app and no account for the person reporting
  • The machine is identified by its QR code: no typing IDs
  • Issue categories that match your equipment
  • Photo and note optional, never required
  • Timestamped history per machine
  • Reports e-mailed to the team responsible

FAQ

Questions about PODFY Report

Do the people reporting need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the form opens in the browser. No app, no login, no account.

Can the same QR code be used again?

Yes. The code stays on the machine and every scan starts a new, separate report, so it works for the next problem too.

Is a photo required?

No. At launch a photo will be optional: choosing what is wrong is enough, and a short note or photo can be added when it helps.

Does PODFY use the phone's location?

It does not need to. The machine and its location are known from the QR code, so a report works without location permission.

Can reports go straight into our ticketing or maintenance system?

At launch every report is e-mailed to the addresses you choose and kept in the PODFY portal. Direct forwarding to other systems is on the roadmap; tell us which system you use.

When is it available and what does it cost?

PODFY Report is in development with pilot places available. Pricing is set per project for now; ask for a proposal.

Where is report data stored?

Reports and photos are stored in the EU on the same platform as PODFY's delivery records. E-mail delivery uses the subprocessors listed on the Trust page.
