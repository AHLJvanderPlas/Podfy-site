Problems get noticed. They rarely get reported well.

The person standing in front of a broken machine is usually not the person who can fix it. They have to find a phone number on a faded sticker, explain which machine they mean and describe the problem from memory. Most give up. The rest send an e-mail without a machine number.

PODFY QR Reporting puts a QR code on every asset. Scanning it opens a short form that already knows the asset and its location. The reporter only says what is wrong. Your team receives a structured report it can act on, and every report stays in a history per asset.

It runs on the platform that already handles proof of delivery for logistics teams, with exactly the same functions: no app, your own branding, notifications, webhooks, EU storage and a searchable portal.

  • No app and no account for the person reporting
  • Every asset identified by its own QR code
  • Issue categories that match your equipment
  • Photo required, optional or off: your choice
  • Reports by e-mail and by webhook
  • Timestamped history per asset

Not an emergency channel. A QR report is for faults and observations. Fire, electrical hazards and injuries go through your local emergency procedures first.

FAQ

Questions about QR Reporting

Do the people reporting need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the form opens in the browser. No app, no login, no account.

Can the same QR code be used again?

Yes. The code stays on the asset and every scan starts a new, separate report, so it works for the next problem too.

Is a photo required?

You decide. Per QR form you choose whether a photo is required, optional or switched off.

How do we add our machines or assets?

Download the asset template, fill in your asset numbers, names and locations, and upload it. Every asset number becomes the reference on its own QR code.

Do we get stickers for the machines?

Yes. PODFY generates a QR code and a printable sticker per asset, with your logo and your own text, for example "Scan to report an issue".

Does PODFY use the phone's location?

It does not need to. The asset and its location are known from the QR code, so a report works without location permission.

Can reports go straight into our ticketing or maintenance system?

Yes. Reports are e-mailed to the addresses you choose and can be sent to your ticketing or maintenance system with a webhook. An open API for your own integrations is coming.

What does QR Reporting cost?

Pricing depends on the number of assets and reports. Ask for a proposal and we will set it up for your situation.

Where is report data stored?

Reports and photos are stored in the EU on the same platform as PODFY's delivery records. E-mail delivery uses the subprocessors listed on the Trust page.
