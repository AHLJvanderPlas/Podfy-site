Every machine is a small shop without staff.

Vending fleets are spread over stations, offices, schools and sports clubs. When something goes wrong, there is nobody to tell. Customers do not know who operates the machine, and a phone number on a sticker gets few calls.

PODFY Report gives every machine a QR code. A customer scans it, picks what is wrong and sends the report. Your route planner sees which machine needs attention and why, before the next visit.

PODFY routes the report. Refunds, payment reversals and customer replies stay in your own process.

  • Every machine identified by its own QR code
  • Categories for stuck products, payment and stock
  • Optional photo of the slot or the display
  • Reports e-mailed to the route or service team
  • History per machine across all your locations
  • No app and no account for customers
PODFY Report does not handle refunds or payment reversals and does not promise customers a response time. Tell customers on the QR label how refunds work in your organisation.

FAQ

Questions from vending machines teams

Can customers request a refund through the report?

The report can include what happened, but PODFY does not process refunds. Your own refund process stays in charge; you can mention it on the QR label.

Do we need one QR code per machine?

Yes, every machine gets its own code, so each report is linked to the right machine and location without the customer typing anything.

Can the same QR code be used again?

Yes. The code stays on the machine and every scan starts a new, separate report, so it works for the next problem too.

When is it available and what does it cost?

PODFY Report is in development with pilot places available. Pricing is set per project for now; ask for a proposal.
