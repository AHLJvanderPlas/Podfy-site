The facilities delivery documentation problem

Facilities teams receive high volumes of deliveries, maintenance supplies, equipment, consumables, contractor materials, often across multiple buildings or sites. Paper delivery notes accumulate in drawers, get lost, and become unmanageable when a supplier query or insurance claim requires documentation from six months ago.

Digital capture at delivery solves the retrieval problem. A GPS-stamped photo at each handover creates a searchable archive that any authorised team member can access, by supplier, delivery date, building reference, or purchase order, without file-searching or manual scanning.

Podfy requires no app installation for delivery staff and no system integration for procurement teams. The link is sent with the delivery instruction; the record appears in the portal. Paper notes become optional confirmation rather than the primary record.

  • No paper filing: all records digital and searchable
  • Retrieve any delivery by supplier, date, or reference
  • GPS confirms delivery to correct building or zone
  • Condition at handover documented: protects against damage claims
  • No app: works for any delivery driver
  • 7-year EU archive for facilities compliance records

How facilities teams use Podfy

Use case 01

Deliveries to different buildings or zones all captured in one portal, organised by building reference. Facilities manager sees all; individual buildings see only theirs.

Use case 02

Supplier query resolution

When a supplier queries a delivery — "was this received?" — the answer is in the portal: photo, GPS, timestamp, linked to the PO. Resolution in minutes.

Insurance and compliance
