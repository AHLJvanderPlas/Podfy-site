Why shippers need a POD standard

Shippers working with multiple carriers face a documentation fragmentation problem. Each carrier uses their own system, or paper, which means delivery evidence arrives in different formats, at different times, in different places.

Finance teams chase POD before approving freight invoices. Procurement teams can't compare carrier performance because the data isn't standardised. Operations teams spend hours reconstructing delivery timelines when disputes arise.

Podfy gives shippers a single standard: one link format, one portal, one export. Carriers don't need to change their systems, they just use the link. The shipper gets GPS-stamped digital POD from every carrier, regardless of their technology stack.

  • Single POD standard across all carriers
  • GPS-stamped: location verified, not just asserted
  • POD available before invoice approval cycle
  • Dispute evidence built-in: no reconstruction needed
  • Carrier performance visible by delivery reference
  • EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant archive

No carrier onboarding required

Carriers open a link. No integration, no account, no training. The shipper defines the standard.

How shippers deploy Podfy

Step 01

Embed in dispatch

Add the Podfy upload link — or QR code — to your standard dispatch instructions or waybill template. No change to carrier workflows required.

Step 02

Carrier uploads at delivery

The driver opens the link, photographs the delivery or CMR. GPS-stamped record appears in your portal. Works for any carrier, any phone.

Step 03

Approve invoices with confidence

Finance team retrieves POD by PO number before approving the freight invoice. No more chasing carriers for documentation.
