The carrier POD problem

Carriers running mixed networks — employed drivers plus subcontractors — face a documentation inconsistency problem. Employed drivers can be trained on a system. Subcontractors use their own phones, may not install apps, and submit paper CMRs days late or not at all.

Shippers increasingly require GPS-stamped digital POD within hours of delivery. When subcontractors can't deliver this, the carrier faces disputes, delayed payment approvals, and strained shipper relationships.

Podfy solves this with a link-based approach. You generate a unique upload link per shipment (or per route). The driver opens it on any phone — no account, no installation — takes a photo, and the GPS-stamped record appears in your portal within seconds.

  • No app — works on any smartphone from 2017
  • GPS captured at moment of camera fire
  • Photo available in portal within seconds
  • Works for employed drivers and subcontractors equally
  • Driver self-copy: driver receives their own branded PDF
  • Your brand, your colours on every driver's upload page
  • Shipper-exportable PDF audit packages

How it works for carriers

Step 01

Generate the link

Create a Podfy upload link for the shipment reference — via the portal, API, or batch CSV. The link encodes the PO number, route, or CMR reference automatically.

Step 02

Send to driver

Include the link in the dispatch message — WhatsApp, SMS, or printed on the waybill as a QR code. No separate system, no additional step for dispatch.

Step 03

Driver uploads at delivery

Driver opens the link, photographs the CMR or delivery point. GPS and timestamp captured. Takes under 30 seconds.

Step 04

Record in portal

Photo and GPS data appear in your Podfy portal immediately. Searchable by reference, date, route, or driver. Exportable as PDF for shipper submission or audit.

Step 05

Driver gets their copy

If the driver enters their email at upload, they receive a branded PDF confirmation automatically — their own record. They can also view their full delivery history at delivery.podfy.net via magic link.
