The retail distribution documentation problem

Warehouse-to-store distribution is high-frequency and high-friction. Store managers report shortages or damage after the driver has gone. The carrier disputes the claim. The retailer's procurement team is stuck between two parties, neither of whom has usable evidence.

This cycle — shortage claim, carrier dispute, write-off or credit note — repeats across the industry. It exists because documentation at the point of handover is inconsistent: some stores sign CMRs, others don't, and neither captures condition or quantity at the moment of delivery.

Podfy closes this with a photo at handover. The driver photographs the delivery — goods in place, quantity visible, any damage noted. The store manager sees the same record. Both parties work from the same evidence. Disputes resolve in hours, not weeks.

  • Photo evidence at handover — condition and quantity visible
  • Shortage and damage codes captured at time of delivery
  • Store manager and logistics team see the same record
  • GPS confirms delivery location — correct store, correct bay
  • Dispute resolution accelerated from weeks to hours
  • Works for any carrier in your distribution network

How retail teams use Podfy

Use case 01

Use case 02

Shortage and damage capture
