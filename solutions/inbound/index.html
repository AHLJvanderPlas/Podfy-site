Why inbound documentation matters

Supplier delivery disputes are among the most common in procurement — and among the hardest to resolve after the fact. Goods are signed for at the dock, move into the warehouse, and shortages or damage are discovered days later during put-away. By then, the carrier has left, the goods have been handled, and the damage could have occurred anywhere.

The solution is documentation at the moment of receipt. When the receiving team photographs the delivery at the dock — condition visible, quantity countable — the record establishes what arrived and in what state, before anything enters the warehouse system.

Podfy enables this with a link the receiving team opens at the dock door. No scanner integration, no WMS connection required. The photo and GPS appear in the portal linked to the delivery reference. Procurement teams retrieve by PO number when a supplier dispute arises.

  • Photo at dock — condition before goods enter warehouse
  • Shortage and damage codes at time of receipt
  • GPS confirms correct receiving location
  • Searchable by PO, supplier, date, or SKU reference
  • No WMS integration required — works standalone
  • Export by supplier for procurement reviews and claims

How inbound teams use Podfy

Use case 01

Dock receipt documentation

Receiving team member opens the Podfy link for the expected PO, photographs the delivery at the dock. Condition and quantity visible before goods are moved. Record in portal within seconds.

Use case 02

Shortage and damage recording

Any discrepancy is noted with the issue code at receipt — before goods move to put-away. The supplier claim is initiated with contemporaneous photo evidence, not retrospective account.

Use case 03

Supplier performance tracking

All inbound deliveries in one portal, searchable by supplier. Identify patterns in shortage frequency, damage rates, or late delivery by carrier over any time period.
