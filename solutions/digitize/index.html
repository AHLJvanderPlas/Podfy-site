The paper-to-digital transition problem

Most logistics operations know they need to move off paper. The barrier isn't motivation — it's the transition risk. A hard cutover that requires all drivers to use a new system on day one always has a compliance tail. Some drivers don't adopt. Routes miss documentation. The first week of digital is worse than the last week of paper.

Podfy's parallel-running approach eliminates this risk. The Podfy link is added to existing dispatch communications alongside the paper process. Drivers who use it get digital capture. Drivers who don't continue with paper as before. No one is forced to change on day one.

Within 2–4 weeks, most operations see 70–90% of drivers uploading digitally without active intervention. The paper process phases out naturally as digital completion rates increase. There is no cutover; there is no single point of failure.

  • Parallel running — paper and digital simultaneously
  • No hard cutover — paper phases out gradually
  • No driver training required — link-based, any phone
  • Digital completion rate visible in portal from day one
  • Typical 95%+ digital completion within 4 weeks
  • CMR originals retained where legally required

No cutover. No compliance gap.

The transition timeline

Week 1–2

Parallel running

Podfy link added to existing dispatch messages. Drivers who use it get instant digital capture. Paper process unchanged. No training, no system change, no pressure to adopt.

Week 3–4

Measure and adjust

Check digital completion rates in portal. Identify routes or drivers still on paper. Send a single follow-up. Most operations reach 70–90% digital by week 4 without active intervention.

Month 2+

Phase out paper

Once digital completion is stable above 95%, formally phase out the paper return process. Retain CMR originals for cross-border compliance; the digital archive handles everything else.
